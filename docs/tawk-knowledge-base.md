## Humidor Care & Setup

Proper care keeps cigars in perfect condition. General best practices:

- **Season a new humidor before use.** A new wooden humidor should be "seasoned" (allowing the Spanish cedar to absorb humidity) before you store cigars in it, so it holds humidity properly.
- **Aim for stable conditions** — the classic target many collectors use is around **70% relative humidity and ~70°F**, though preferences vary.
- **Check your hygrometer** and keep your humidification device charged/filled.
- **Keep it out of direct sunlight and away from heat sources.**

Full, detailed guidance: https://dunnluxuryselections.com/pages/care-guides

For product-specific setup questions, point customers to the Care Guides page, and for anything unclear, offer to connect them with our team.
