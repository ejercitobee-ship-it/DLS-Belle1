Walk-In Humidors

A walk-in humidor is a fully climate-controlled room — a dedicated environment where cedar, light, and design converge to protect and elevate every cigar. We design and build each one around your space, your aesthetic, and your collection: private cigar rooms, retail lounges, members' clubs, hotels, and restaurants.

Spanish Cedar Interior

Walls and shelving lined in premium Spanish cedar — prized for its aroma, humidity regulation, and natural protection against tobacco pests.

Insulation & Sealing

Full insulation with vapor barriers, airtight doors, and precision-sealed joints for maximum climate stability.

Airflow Engineering

Hidden chambers, grilles, and double cedar back panels guarantee even humidity in every corner of the room.

Professional Climate Control

Commercial-grade humidification with stainless ducting — independent tanks or plumbed supply, with clear calibration and setup guidance for your team.

Cigar-Safe LED Lighting

Cold, low-heat LEDs with high color rendering; ambient lighting options elevate presentation.

Custom Configurations

Shelves and trays for singles, racks for full boxes, glass display areas — modules designed around your real inventory.
