## IDEAL STORAGE CONDITIONS
- **Temperature:** 65–72°F (18–22°C)
- **Humidity:** 65–72% RH
- Electronic cabinets (Raching, Reagan) maintain these automatically at 16–22°C ±1°C, 60–75% RH ±1%
- Use distilled water only for humidifiers — tap water causes mineral buildup
