## 2. Scope of Work

2.1 DLS will design and hand-build one (1) custom Humidor according to the specifications set out in the Order Summary and any attached design sheet, renderings, or written approvals (collectively, the "**Specifications**").

2.2 **Design approval.** DLS will provide the Client with a final design confirmation (specifications and, where applicable, a rendering or reference image) prior to commencing fabrication. The Client must approve the design in writing (email is acceptable). **The build clock and the production timeline in Section 4 begin only after both (a) the required payment has cleared and (b) the Client has approved the final design.**

2.3 **Handcrafted nature.** The Humidor is a handmade, made-to-order luxury item. Natural variation in wood grain, color, and figure is inherent and expected, and does not constitute a defect. Renderings and samples are representative, not exact.

2.4 **Change requests.** Any change to the Specifications after design approval must be requested in writing and is subject to DLS's acceptance, a revised price, and a revised timeline. Changes may require an additional deposit.

2.5 **What is included.** The Product is the custom-built humidor cabinet only. Unless expressly listed in the Specifications, accessories such as hygrometers, humidification devices, humidity pouches, and cigars are **not included** and are the Client's responsibility to obtain. Care and seasoning instructions are provided separately (the "Care Guide").

## 3. Price and Payment

The Client selects **one** of the following payment methods in the Order Summary.

### 3A. Direct Payment

3A.1 **Initial payment (70%).** To commence the project, the Client pays **seventy percent (70%)** of the Total Product Price. Work does not begin until this payment clears.

3A.2 **Final payment (30%) + shipping.** The remaining **thirty percent (30%)** of the Total Product Price, **plus all shipping charges**, becomes due once the Humidor is complete and DLS has prepared the logistics/shipping quote. DLS will invoice the Client for this balance. **DLS will not ship or release the Humidor until the 30% balance and all shipping charges are paid in full.**

3A.3 Shipping charges are quoted separately from the Product Price because they depend on the final destination, carrier, insurance, and packaging, which are determined near completion. See Section 5.

3B.5 Once DLS has received cleared funds from the Financing Provider for the required commencement amount, the project proceeds under this Agreement as if paid directly.
